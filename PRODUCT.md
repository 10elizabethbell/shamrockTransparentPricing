## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, system fonts, hosted on GitHub Pages. Chosen because the pitch-site pipeline ships every prospect this way: fast on cellular, opens inside Facebook's in-app browser, trivial to hand over.

## Users

- **Snowbirds, mostly 60+**, who live in New Jersey for the summer and Florida for the winter. They fly; they need their own car at the other end. They are deciding who to trust with their car, usually on a phone. *(age skew from the brief's builder notes)*
- **Their adult children** researching on a parent's behalf. *(inferred)*
- **Ed Brannagan, the owner**, who sees this page first. It's given to him free; it has to look like his business done properly.

## Product Purpose

A one-page website for Shamrock Transportation, a one-man driveaway service: Ed drives a customer's own car between New Jersey and Florida while they fly. The page gets a snowbird to call Ed (or text him an estimate request) with their route, dates and car. Success: the phone rings.

## Positioning

Transparent pricing. The industry's top complaint is bait-and-switch quotes; Shamrock's card says "Very Reasonable Rates". The page's whole argument: one flat rate for your route, said up front, no surprises. Second differentiator: driveaway means the customer can pack the car full of winter things, which trailer shippers don't allow.

## Operating Context

- Customers book by phone. Ed's cell is the business line.
- Seasonal: south in the fall, north in late spring.
- Ed is the only known driver.

## Evidence on Hand

None public. No photos, reviews, testimonials, rates, insurance or credentials. Do not fabricate any of them. Competitor rates in the brief are market context only, never displayed.

## Product Principles

1. Say the price plainly or say plainly when you'll say it. Never a fake number.
2. The phone number is never more than a thumb away.
3. Big type, big targets: the reader is 60+ on a phone.
4. Answer the objection honestly, including when a truck carrier is the better choice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience skews 60+: 18px+ body text, 56px+ primary buttons, strong contrast, no meaning carried by motion alone, reduced-motion respected.
